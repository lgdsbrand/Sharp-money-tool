How to read a card

Each row is one game. Here is a sample, with every part explained.

6HEAVY SHARP FADE PUBLIC
  1. Game — the matchup, league and start time (your local time).
  2. Sharp side — which market the sharp money is in (Moneyline Spread Total), the side it is on (a team, a team with its spread like SF +3.5, or Over 44.5), and what share of that market's money it is. The Market board shows all three markets for each game with the dollars on each side.
  3. Exchange money — dollars on each exchange: the sharp side / the other side. K = Kalshi, N = Novig, PX = ProphetX.
  4. Public on this side — (moneyline only for now) the share of DraftKings bets (tickets) and handle (dollars) on the same team. A low number means the public is on the other side.
  5. Best EV — the best price compared with Pinnacle's fair price. Green and positive means a better price than fair.
  6. Flags — quick signals, explained below.
HEAVY SHARPmost of the exchange money is on one team
BIG MONEYa lot of money in the market in total
FADE PUBLICsharp money and the public are on opposite sides
+EVa price better than Pinnacle's fair price
MODEL AGREESour model picks the same team as the sharp money

Tip: use the − / + buttons by Min liquidity to hide small markets. This is information, not betting advice.
